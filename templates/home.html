How it works

Three steps from your lecturer to your phone

1. Your lecturer uploads the scores

Lecturers enter results for the courses they teach. They can do one student at a time, or upload a whole class in a single file.

2. The department approves them

A department admin checks every score before it goes out. Nothing reaches you until it has been approved.

3. You get an alert

As soon as your result is approved, we send it to your phone and your email. You do not have to keep checking.
